# BrewPath site glossary

The words the site's copy, code and docs share. Terms the app also uses keep
the app's meaning; the app's own glossary is `GLOSSARY.md` in the
[brewpath repo](https://github.com/maximsan/brewpath).

**Foundations** — the paid course: every module past the free lessons, the
full dictionary, unlimited saved terms and the Studio.
_Avoid_: Plus, BrewPath Plus, Premium

**Coffee Card** — a collectible card. A lesson's card is earned by finishing
that lesson with a perfect score. Each module also awards one card, its
Module Reward. Example: _The Coffee Cherry_.

**Module Reward** — the Coffee Card a whole module awards, earned when every
lesson in the module has a perfect score. Its title can contain "Field Guide",
e.g. _Beans Field Guide_.
_Avoid_: Field Guide, as the name for the kind of card

**Mood** — one of the two colour themes. Cupping is light; Dark Roast is dark.
Every colour has a value for each.
_Avoid_: theme, in copy

**Roasty** — the coffee-bean mascot.

**Selling model** — how Foundations is sold: once for good (one-time), as a
monthly or yearly subscription, or both, the learner's choice (hybrid).
_Avoid_: pricing arm, monetization model (the app's code names)

**Share image** — the picture a link to the site shows when it is shared in a
chat or social app. A Coffee Card page has its own, showing that card.
_Avoid_: share preview, OG image

**Website privacy page** — the site's own privacy notice, covering visit
counting. The app's notice is the Privacy Policy.
_Avoid_: privacy policy, when the website page is meant
